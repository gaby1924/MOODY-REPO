# MOODY the mood logging app for when you're feeling moody.

# ABOUT
MOODY is an application that allows users to log their mood levels,
their medication intake, their intent to harm, and other symptoms they
may be experiencing. The app saves the daily logs and creates a MOODY report.
The user's report will consist of all their logged entries and will be displayed
in the form of a line graph. The future intention of this application is to save a user's
data indefinitely (and can be edited by the user), to save the user's medication(s)
information and other medical information, and to allow the user to share with their 
medical team (primary care physician, therapist, psychiatrist, etc.). Until then, the 
application stores data locally.
